EMO-AMPLIFIER

Integral AI Pipeline For Emotionally Expressive Text-To-Speech Synthesis

Hanhee Lee, Sky Qiao, Emmanuel Ko, Pushya Jain,
Amirhossein Hajavi, Yuanhao Yu, Irina Kezele

Abstract

Present-day emotional TTS models increasingly adopt LLM backbones and related progressive training methodologies. However, diverse, high-fidelity emotional and expressive speech data are dramatically scarce. This reduces the effectiveness of model training and post-optimization. To tackle these issues, we propose to first develop a data pipeline for text and speech generation with multiple control variables of interest, to produce human-like speech data. Second, capitalizing on the resulting realistic synthetic data, we finetune and post-optimize base LLM-TTS models, and train new powerful reward models for emotional expressiveness scoring. Our experimental results demonstrate clear accuracy gain of the proposed reward models and considerably enhanced speech emotional expressiveness with our methodological design.
